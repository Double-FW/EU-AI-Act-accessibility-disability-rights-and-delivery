## Source records for local decisions

For each adopted requirement retain ID, title, URL, publisher, exact article/clause, language, edition/effective date, retrieval date, authority type, applicable jurisdiction, amendment/citation status, excerpt or qualified paraphrase, interpretation, owner and review trigger. Keep source text and commentary distinguishable.

If a source is unavailable, preserve its identity, affected claims, last verified status and the next evidence needed. Locate an authoritative alternative; never fabricate content from a title. Do not silently convert “not verified” into “does not exist”.

Linked sources retain their own rights. The repository's MIT licence covers its original material, not standards, external content or another repository's files.
