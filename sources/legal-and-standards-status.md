# Dated legal and standards status

**Reference date: 11 November 2026 · Version 0.1.0**

This file owns shared dates and status; other guides summarise them. Recheck before a live legal decision or public claim. An amendment proposal, political agreement, published regulation and applicable obligation are different states.

## Legal reference

The reference is Regulation 2024/1689 as amended by Regulation 2026/1744, published 24 July 2026 and in force 27 July 2026. The EUR-Lex consolidation dated 27 July 2026 and Commission implementation page were revisited for this package. The authentic published acts control. No later amendment is asserted by this release.
