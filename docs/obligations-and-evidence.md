## Keep the legal claim scoped

The table focuses on operator delivery. Institutional governance, notified-body designation, delegated powers and procedural articles require the competent authority's current instructions when they affect the use; this package does not appoint or substitute for those bodies. Other Annex I instruments, IVDR and national rules need their own source record when applicable.

Article 16(l)'s express accessibility obligation should not be dismissed simply because a high-risk use is not independently a consumer service under the EAA. Equally, do not assume every EAA/WAD administrative procedure transfers wholesale to every AI provider. Obtain a reasoned mapping of the accessibility requirements and scope.

## Connect evidence without flattening it

One keyboard observation can support several scoped requirements. It cannot establish data fairness or legal classification. One FRIA can reference a DPIA, but each instrument's coverage remains visible. A supplier report can support a control while integration evidence remains missing.

Maintain many-to-many links from legal duty to adopted requirement, design/control, test method, execution, issue, decision and report. Preserve revisions and limitations at each link. Do not count planned tests, blank templates or mappings as executed evidence.
