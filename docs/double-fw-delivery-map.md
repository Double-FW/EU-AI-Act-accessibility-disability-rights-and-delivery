# Double-FW delivery and testing map

**Document DM · Version 0.1.0 · 11 November 2026**

These public knowledge sources support methods, actions and evaluation. They are not legislation, harmonised standards or proof of a deployed compliance service. Retrieve the owning guide/test, version and limitations before operational reuse. Do not copy private snapshots or assume another repository's licence is MIT because this repository is.

| ID / public resource | Approach and action | Testing/evidence contribution | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D01 [Accessibility Programme Runbooks](https://github.com/Double-FW/Accessibility_Programme_Runbooks) | Coordinate employee/customer programmes, named decisions and shared lifecycle. Use its design/brand and human/AI responsibility files. | Trace foundations/components/journeys to methods, actual observations and decision evidence. | Documentation and draft operating method; not an implemented orchestration/enforcement system. |

| D13 [Closed Caption / Subtitles](https://github.com/Double-FW/Closed-Caption-Subtitles) | Apply caption editorial practice to dialogue, speakers and meaningful audio. | Correctness, completeness, timing, presentation and audience understanding. | Caption existence or syntax pass does not establish access; use actual language/platform specifications. |
| D14 [POUR for Branding Systems and CX](https://github.com/Double-FW/POUR-for-Branding-Systems-and-CX) | Preserve identification, trust, expectations, value and inclusive resonance across modalities. | Recognition, comprehension and confidence research alongside technical checks. | Brand/emotional framing does not alter WCAG definitions or create statutory requirements. |
| D15 [Executive and Organisational Commitments](https://github.com/Double-FW/Executive-and-Organisational-Commitments) | Turn evidence into owned, funded commitments and renewal decisions. | Trace public claims to source, delivery record and actual effect. | A pledge without resources/evidence is not assurance. |
| D16 [Global Regulatory Requirements](https://github.com/Double-FW/Global_Accessiblity_Regulatory_Requirements) | Route multi-jurisdiction scope, legal profiles and reporting types. | Separate technical findings, functional outcomes and statutory outputs. | Refresh current primary law; a global overview does not decide a local case. |
| D17 [Little Book of Accessibility](https://github.com/Double-FW/The-Little-Book-of-Accessibility) | Frame inclusive strategy, design, research and evidence-aware agent assistance. | Connect designed experiences, participation and operational outcomes. | Secondary guidance; retrieved text is not runtime permission or a normative standard. |
