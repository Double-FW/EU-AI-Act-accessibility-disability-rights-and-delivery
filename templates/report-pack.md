# Report pack field guide

**Blank templates · Use with the reporting guide · Not an official statutory form**

## A. Management decision report

1. **Identity:** entity, accountable/reporting owner, period, date/version and intended audience.
2. **Scope:** systems/uses, people, surfaces, countries, versions and exclusions/limits.
3. **Current legal position:** applicable duties/dates, unverified classification and changed sources.
4. **Evidence:** separate interface access, output quality, fair treatment, human control and remedy findings; coverage, method, sample and uncertainty.
5. **Material issues:** actual/possible harm, nonconformities, risk, owner, correction and retest.
6. **Operating capability:** supplier cooperation, literacy, oversight, support, resources and dependencies.
7. **Decisions/actions:** exact decision needed, authorised function, options, consequence, due date and reason.
8. **Approval/distribution:** actual authority, approved text/version, audience and evidence of handoff.

Initial status: Draft, with no actual service findings.

## B. Statutory or authority handoff cover record

Record legal provision, entity/role, jurisdiction, competent authority/verified recipient, required official format/template, trigger, awareness/effective dates, deadline calculation, exact system/version and information requested. Attach actual evidence and qualifications using the permitted secure method.

Record authorised reviewer/sender, version, approval, actual send/receipt, reference number if genuinely returned, follow-up dates and corrections. Keep Draft, Approved, Sent and Received distinct. Do not invent a regulator endpoint, template, contact or receipt.

For FRIA notification include the applicable Article 27 content and actual required route. For incidents use the chronology in the decision/incident record and the correct clock. For conformity/registration follow the actual legal mechanism rather than repurpose this cover record as the required document.

## C. Public service/AI/accessibility information plan

Define audience and access needs, service/AI purpose, required disclosure/marking paragraph, known capabilities/limits, appropriate help/rights routes, applicable EAA/WAD service-information format, assessed scope/date and truthful limitations.

Record source/claim evidence, clear language, alternate formats, structured document/web presentation, first-interaction/exposure placement where relevant, actual accessibility evaluation, authorised publication and review trigger. Machine-readable marking remains a separate technical requirement where applicable.

## D. Individual response plan

Record protected case ID, service/version, request type, actual right and timeframe, chosen communication mode, facts/output/decision evidence, competent reviewer, explanation/correction/remedy, accessible response and next route. Do not demand unnecessary medical disclosure. Separate an explanation of the actual decision from speculative model reasoning.
