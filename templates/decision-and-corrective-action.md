# Decision, issue, corrective action and incident record

**Blank field guide · WF08/WF10–WF12 · No approval implied**

## Decision

Capture decision ID; entity/service/use/version; proposed action; applicable duties; actual evidence and limits; alternatives; unresolved risks/nonconformities; legal/policy departure distinctions; competent reviewers; authorised decision-maker/delegation; authenticated decision/date; effective period; conditions; review/stop triggers; and linked reports.

Possible decision states include Draft, Ready for decision, Authorised for stated scope, Not authorised, Conditional internal decision, Superseded and Withdrawn. A conditional internal decision does not establish statutory conformity. Record what the decision permits and what remains unresolved.

## Issue and correction

- Issue ID, observed barrier/harm and source: Not supplied.
- Affected people, tasks, environments, versions and legal/requirement links: Not supplied.
- Observation versus risk hypothesis, severity method and evidence: Not supplied.
- Immediate protection/support and accountable/responsible owner: Not supplied.
- Cause, correction plan, target and dependencies: Not supplied.
- Changed versions, retest method/result and residual limits: Not supplied.
- Closure decision and verification: Not supplied; ticket completion alone is insufficient.

## Incident addendum

Record an absolute chronology with time zone: event, first awareness, suspicion/causal assessment, classification, protective action, notification, follow-up, correction and restart. Identify actual actor, applicable serious-incident definition, threshold, exact provision, recipient and deadline calculation separately for each legal route.

Add affected systems/people, known and unknown facts, secured evidence, reporting owner/authority, initial/incomplete report where relevant, follow-up plan and actual submission/receipt references. Preserve privacy and evidence integrity. Do not let a blank form delay an applicable notification clock.

## Agent/action delegation, where relevant

Record purpose, authorised person, permitted targets/actions/data, environment, access limits, consequential-action confirmations, logging, duration/volume limits, stop conditions, verifier and recovery. Retrieved documents do not enlarge delegation. A model-generated approval is not authentication.

## Retirement/restart

Link safe-state conditions, user communication, accessible alternative/migration, records/data retention/deletion, supplier actions and authenticated restart/closure. Preserve previous findings and effective periods.
