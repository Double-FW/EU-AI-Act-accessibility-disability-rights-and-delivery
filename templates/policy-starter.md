# Human-centred AI and accessibility policy starter

**Proposed text for adaptation and adoption · Not an adopted policy**

Before adoption complete the organisation, scope, owner, approving authority, version, effective date, resources, review arrangements and links to actual procedures. Distinguish sourced legal duties from stronger local choices. Use WF03 and RB03.

## Proposed commitments

1. We identify the purpose, affected people, accountable owner, operator roles, scope and evidence for each AI use. We reassess material changes rather than rely on a supplier or product label.
2. We prevent applicable prohibited practices and promptly stop/escalate suspected prohibited or unsafe use. An internal exception cannot waive law.
3. We support accessible, role-appropriate AI literacy, including limitations, disability inclusion, privacy, oversight and remedy. We use practice and feedback to improve capability.
4. We include accessibility, fair treatment, output quality, human control and remedy in design, procurement, implementation and operation. Applicable legal requirements are recorded separately from additional service ambitions.
5. We consider relevant disabled people and non-users in research and decisions, provide accessible participation and respect disclosure choices. We do not infer diagnosis or capacity from behaviour or preferences.
6. We use minimum necessary data with lawful processing arrangements. Sensitive-data bias work follows all applicable conditions and safeguards. Accessibility preferences are not an excuse for surveillance or unauthorised model training.
7. We assign competent people with real authority to relevant oversight. Tools, instructions, intervention and recovery must work for the operators who use them.
8. We provide applicable AI notices, output marking and decision information clearly and accessibly. Capabilities, uncertainty and availability of human support are communicated honestly.
9. We require supplier evidence and cooperation proportionate to our role and intended use, and evaluate the configured integration before relying on it.
10. We maintain accessible UX and branding systems, layered assessment, complete-journey evaluation and traceable correction/retest. A score or clean scan does not establish universal accessibility or fairness.
11. We fulfil applicable conformity, registration, impact assessment, information, reporting and authority-cooperation duties through authorised owners and current requirements.
12. We offer accessible support/challenge routes, monitor harm and exclusion, preserve evidence and provide effective corrective action. Retirement and migration protect people and address records/data responsibly.

## Local procedure and control table

For each commitment add: applicable law/policy source, control, responsible/accountable functions, resources, workflow, evidence, reporting audience and review trigger. State which support and service alternatives actually exist. Do not promise an unstaffed human service.
