# Assessment plan and execution record

**Blank template · WF07 · Every execution starts Not tested**

## Plan the scope

Record plan/requirement IDs, purpose, legal or adopted-policy authority, applicable clauses, actual system/model/configuration, surfaces, languages, user/operator journeys and dependencies. Specify supported OS/browser/AT/device versions, input/output modes, dataset/content fixtures, real-model sampling and repetitions, specialist competence and participant coverage.

For each test define objective, applicability, setup, input/steps, expected observable outcome, method/version, automation coverage/residual manual work, evidence storage, privacy arrangement, responsible evaluator and acceptance/decision route. Link local AI-A scenarios to actual owning canonical tests without changing their namespaces.

## Result vocabulary

| Result | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Not tested | No valid execution evidence exists for this scope. |
| Pass | All applicable assertions in the scoped case meet its expected outcomes with valid evidence. |
| Fail | One or more applicable assertions demonstrably fail; identify each. |
| Blocked | A dependency/access/setup issue prevents the assessment. |
| Tool error | The method/tool malfunctioned; not a product pass/fail. |
| Cannot determine | Evidence is insufficient or ambiguous despite attempted assessment. |
| Not applicable | Preconditions are not met, with an evidence-backed rationale. |

Keep applicability, implementation status, test result, research finding and approval separate. Retain compound sub-results and raw results. A criterion exception, legal exemption or policy departure needs its own reasoned record; do not silently weaken expected outcomes.

## Evaluation and decision limits

For generative trials report repetitions, variability, meaningful errors, data/context coverage and uncertainty. For research distinguish observation, interpretation, participant view and recommendation. For fairness review validity/proxies/accommodation and subgroup limits. Neither generated scenarios nor a simulated pass supplies participant evidence.

Use actual integration tests after fixtures. Native apps, physical products, media, speech and model/data questions require their own competent methods. Reference an approved safety protocol before any physical/medical failure exercise.
