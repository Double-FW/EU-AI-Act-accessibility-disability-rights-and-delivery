# Risk, fundamental rights and data assessment record

**Blank template · WF05 · Initial state: Not assessed**

This is a working field guide, not an official FRIA questionnaire or DPIA form. Use the actual required template/authority route when applicable. Keep personal, applicant, health and personnel records in approved protected systems.

## Context and assessment applicability

Record system/use/version, purpose, owner, entities/countries, affected people including excluded non-users, relevant surfaces, scope decision, processing and deployment period/frequency, exact legal sources and assessment competence.

Make separate decisions on Article 9 risk-system duties, Article 27 FRIA, GDPR/other DPIA, equality/accommodation assessment, sector/safety assessment and regulated real-world testing. For each retain applicability, rationale, decision-maker, gaps and review trigger.

## FRIA content where required

Capture the processes where the system is used; intended time period/frequency; affected categories/groups; specific risks taking provider information into account; oversight implementation; and governance/complaint measures if risks materialise. Add updates, relevant DPIA cross-references and required notification. Reuse an assessment only with a reasoned match to the actual deployment.

## Data and participation safeguards

Record lawful basis and special-category conditions where relevant, collection purpose, minimum fields, source, recipients/processors/transfers, access, security, retention/deletion, accessible information and rights routes. Pseudonymisation is not anonymisation.

If relying on Article 4a, record strict necessity, why other data cannot work, reuse/security/access safeguards, its no-third-party-access condition, deletion and processing-record rationale. Obtain a qualified privacy assessment before this route is used; consent alone does not substitute for its conditions.

Record accessible recruitment/consent, participant influence, access arrangements, coverage, compensation/support, safeguarding and evidence interpretation. Never manufacture lived-experience evidence with generated personas.

## Outcome

Link actions, tests, issues, findings, limitations, authenticated decisions and reports. Record missing evidence with an owner, consequence and next action. Statuses distinguish Planned, In progress, Completed for stated scope and Reassessment required; none alone means legal approval.
