# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 11 November 2026

- Created human-centred AI Act guidance with role, scope, transparency, model, disability and rights explanations.
- Added broad surface/product coverage, including websites, native apps, APIs, agents, workplace/voice/media systems and regulated products.
- Added twelve governance workflows connecting policy, delivery, evidence, reporting, incidents and change/retirement.
- Added accessibility delivery methods and eighteen local assessment scenarios, with public Double-FW routing and explicit method limits.
- Added thirty-six obligation/evidence routes, parallel-law guidance, reporting and reusable blank records.
- Added five illustrative scenarios, source/status/provenance records, agent instructions and MIT licence.

This initial release contains documentation and templates. It does not supply executed service assessments, operational integrations, national adapters or legal approvals.
